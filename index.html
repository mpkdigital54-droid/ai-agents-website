FAQ

Frequently asked questions about AI Agents

What is an AI agent for business? +

An AI agent for business is an intelligent software assistant that holds natural conversations with customers, qualifies leads, books appointments, and completes tasks automatically — 24/7 without human intervention. Unlike basic chatbots that follow rigid scripts, AI agents understand context and intent to handle complex conversations naturally.

Do I need coding skills to build an AI agent? +

No coding skills are needed whatsoever. AI Agents is a completely no-code platform. Build, customize, and deploy a fully functional AI agent using a visual editor in under 10 minutes, using plain English instructions.

How long does it take to deploy an AI agent? +

Most businesses deploy their first AI agent in under 10 minutes. Choose an industry template, customise the agent's name and personality, upload your business documents, and click Deploy. The agent is immediately live on your website or messaging channels.

How much does AI Agents cost? +

The Starter plan is free forever — 1 agent, 200 conversations/month. Growth is $79/month (5 agents, unlimited conversations, all channels). Business is $249/month (unlimited agents, white-label, API, dedicated manager). Annual billing saves 20%.

Which industries can use AI Agents? +

AI Agents is purpose-built for real estate agencies, e-commerce stores, service businesses (salons, clinics, contractors), healthcare providers, legal firms, and financial services companies. Every industry gets pre-trained templates that are ready to deploy on day one.
