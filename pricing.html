FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I really start for free?
Yes — the Starter plan is free forever, no credit card required. You get 1 agent, 200 conversations a month, and access to 3 industry templates. When you're ready to scale, upgrading takes 30 seconds.
What counts as a "conversation"?
A conversation is a single continuous session between your AI agent and a customer. Whether it lasts 2 messages or 20, it counts as one conversation. Sessions that resume within 24 hours are counted as the same conversation.
Can I cancel or change plans at any time?
Absolutely. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time from your account settings. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of your current billing period. We don't do lock-in contracts.
Do you offer a free trial of paid plans?
Yes — the Growth and Business plans come with a 14-day free trial that includes all features. No credit card is required to start the trial.
Is my business data safe?
We take data security seriously. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We are SOC 2 Type II compliant and GDPR-ready. We never use your business data to train our models.
Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or startups?
Yes — we offer a 40% discount for registered nonprofits and an extended 6-month trial for early-stage startups. Email us at hello@aiagents.com with proof of status.
